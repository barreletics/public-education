Drug sourcing — when your guide says go

DrugBest sourceNotes
Fenbendazole
(Panacur C)		 Tractor Supply · farm & feed store · rural co-op Joe Tippens used this. Brand: Panacur C — 1g packet = 222 mg. ~$8–15. Mix in fatty food. Farm supply is the standard source — skip random Amazon capsule brands.
Mebendazole
(human form)		 Pharmacy with prescription (Vermox / Emverm) · or telehealth MD Same drug family as fenbendazole but human-approved. Care Oncology and some MD protocols use this instead of Panacur. Needs a doctor.
Ivermectin
(human tablets)		 Compounding pharmacy or telehealth doctor · 3, 6, or 12 mg tablets Preferred: human pharma-grade tablets from an MD who writes the Rx. Pills often come in 12 mg — you may need ~18–24 mg/day at ~170 lb depending on protocol (combine tablets).
Ivermectin
(farm / horse paste)		 Farm supply (some use this) Some people use farm formulations — dosing is harder and less precise than tablets. Human tablets from a compounding pharmacy or MD are safer once you have a prescriber. Don't start farm paste without a guide.
Farm vs human: For fenbendazole, farm Panacur C is the normal source (same as Tippens). For ivermectin, human tablets from a doctor/compounder are usually better than guessing farm paste doses. For mebendazole, that's the human Rx path — pharmacy only.

Questions for the oncologist

  1. Confirm high-grade NEC — Ki-67? Small-cell or large-cell type?
  2. First-line chemo — carboplatin + etoposide? How many cycles? When do we start?
  3. Radiation to sinus? Any liver-directed options?
  4. Was DOTATATE PET done? (Lutathera option — often no for high-grade NEC)
  5. Clinical trials at Karmanos for this diagnosis?
  6. We're doing strict keto + GKI tracking — any concerns with chemo?
  7. We're adding fenbendazole + ivermectin on a cycle (dose TBD with metabolic clinician) — interaction concerns with etoposide/platinum?
  8. Which supplements or drugs must we avoid during chemo?
  9. Who on the team helps with nutrition + metabolic support during chemo? Can they co-manage keto/GKI?
  10. How to reach you if side effects spike between visits?

What to say (read aloud if helpful)

"Greg has high-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma, sinus primary, liver and pelvis mets. We want carboplatin/etoposide — that's the main weapon. We're also running metabolic therapy in parallel: strict keto, GKI monitoring, fenbendazole and ivermectin on a cycle (doses being set with a metabolic clinician), plus supplements. Not instead of chemo — to enhance response. We need your help on drug interactions, liver monitoring, and safe timing with chemo cycles."
