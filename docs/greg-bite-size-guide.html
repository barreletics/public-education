Why both lanes work better

Chemo kills dividing cells. Keto + GKI starves tumor fuel. Both together — not either/or.

GKI in plain English
Prick finger → test strip in Keto-Mojo → write glucose and ketones → divide glucose by ketones = GKI.
Target ≤ 3. Check twice a day: morning before food, evening before dinner.
Strips: you need two boxes — glucose strips + ketone strips (both Keto-Mojo brand).

You'll see different numbers online. Dose is mostly agreed; cycle timing is not. Pick one schedule and stay consistent.

Cycled (FBZ + ivermectin together on ON days)

DrugDose on ON days
Fenbendazole222 mg · one Panacur C packet · with fat
Ivermectin12 mg · same ON days as FBZ
Why 222 mg fenbendazole? Joe Tippens dose · one 1g Panacur packet = 222 mg. Some sources say 444–2000 mg for aggressive cancer — start at 222 mg unless you and your metabolic clinician decide otherwise.

Cycle timing — sources disagree (this is normal)

What you may seeScheduleNotes
Joe Tippens / many Facebook groups4 days ON · 10 days OFF14-day repeat cycle
What Greg read6 days ON · 6 days OFF12-day repeat cycle
Makis (high-grade cancers)6 days ON · 1 day OFFMore aggressive schedule

There is no single right answer in the research. Pick one, write it down, stay consistent. Kim suggests Greg consider a consult with Dr. Katsonas — he knows this plan and can help choose cycle timing and tune doses. That runs alongside Karmanos, not instead of it.

Where to buy

Keto-Mojo meter — amazon.com or keto-mojo.com (~$45). Comes with a few strips; order extra.
Glucose strips + ketone strips — separate boxes, Keto-Mojo brand. ~2 checks/day = plan on refills.
Print keto starter sheet · any grocery · eggs, avocado, meat, fish, greens, cheese. Optional app: Carb Manager or Cronometer (not required).
Amazon or grocery — LMNT, Nuun, or salt + potassium. Prevents keto flu.
Apple cider vinegar (any grocery) · matcha or green tea bags (Amazon / grocery)
Dr. Katsonas — Kim recommends a consult to pick cycle timing and tune the protocol
Panacur C — Tractor Supply, Amazon, farm stores (~$8–15). Each 1g packet = 222 mg. Or pre-made capsules (Sanare Lab, etc.) for convenience — same dose, not stronger.
Human 12 mg tablets (online pharmacies / telehealth) preferred. Some use farm formulations — 12 mg on cycle days is the target dose.
Glycinate (best for sleep/cramps) or citrate — Amazon, ~200–400 mg elemental. Avoid magnesium oxide (cheap, poor absorption).
Amazon, Costco, grocery — vitamin D3, E, curcumin. Pill organizer for cycle days.
