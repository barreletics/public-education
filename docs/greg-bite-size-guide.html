What is NEC? (your diagnosis in plain English)

NEC = Neuroendocrine Carcinoma. Neuroendocrine cells exist throughout the body — they normally release hormones in a controlled way. When they become cancerous and grow aggressively, that's NEC.

Greg's specifics:

  • High-grade — fast-dividing, aggressive cells (often high Ki-67 on pathology)
  • Sinonasal primary — started in the sinus / paranasal area
  • Stage IV — spread to liver and pelvis; whole-body treatment is the focus

Why Karmanos matters: High-grade NEC is treated like other aggressive neuroendocrine cancers — usually platinum + etoposide chemo first. Karmanos has a dedicated GI & Neuroendocrine Oncology team for exactly this.

Why chemo + diet together? (the whole point)

Chemo and metabolic therapy attack cancer from different angles — and together they can work better than either alone.

Chemo does Metabolic lane does
Kills rapidly dividing cancer cells (carboplatin + etoposide) Starves tumor fuel — glucose & glutamine — that fast-growing NEC depends on
Systemic attack on mets (liver, pelvis) GKI tracking (glucose ÷ ketones) — measurable proof you're starving fuel; target ≤3
Standard proven first-line for high-grade NEC May sensitize tumors to chemo by lowering the metabolic shield (Seyfried research)
Targets cell division Benzimidazoles (if MD OK) hit microtubules & glutamine — may complement etoposide

Bottom line: Chemo is the hammer. Keto + GKI + approved adjuncts make the cancer less able to feed, hide, and resist. That's why this approach is better together — and why you ask Karmanos to run both lanes with their blessing.
