02

Lane A — Karmanos (Wed Aug 13)

Chemo planning visit. Lead with chemo; tell them about keto + supplements; skip antiparasitics.

Bring

  • Pathology · imaging · symptom list
  • Written list: keto + supplements only (from §03)
  • This page

Ask — diagnosis & chemo

  1. Confirm high-grade NEC — Ki-67? Small-cell or large-cell?
  2. Staging complete — any sites beyond liver + pelvis?
  3. Carboplatin + etoposide — how many cycles, when start?
  4. Radiation? Liver-directed options? Clinical trials?
  5. When restage? What response by cycle 2–3?
  6. After-hours contact if side effects spike?

Tell them — keto & supplements

"We want carboplatin/etoposide as first-line. We're doing strict keto with daily glucose-ketone tracking, plus vitamins on this list. Walk us through the chemo plan and flag anything on our diet or supplements that conflicts."
  1. Strict keto + daily GKI — anything to change during chemo?
  2. Supplement list (§03) — anything to pause with etoposide/platinum?
  3. Liver mets — how will you monitor LFTs and blood counts?
