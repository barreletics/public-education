Lane B — Antiparasitics (optional · outside MD)

Optional · outside MD only. Based on Joe Tippens's fenbendazole dose — other versions exist; this is what the research pointed to.

  • Fenbendazole (Panacur C): 222 mg/day with fat — one 1g packet
  • Ivermectin (human tablets): 18–24 mg/day with fat (~170 lb)
  • Cycle: 6 days on · 1 day off · repeat
