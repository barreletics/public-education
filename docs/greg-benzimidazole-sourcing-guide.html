30-Second Answer for Greg

DrugWhat it isResearch laneWhat Facebook says
Fenbendazole (FBZ) Veterinary dewormer (Panacur C, farm supply). Not FDA-approved for humans. Joe Tippens community protocol (222 mg). Preclinical papers. No completed human cancer trials. "Farm supply is the real protocol." Cheap. Original story.
Mebendazole (MBZ) Human Rx antiparasitic (Vermox/emverm). FDA-approved for worms in people. Seyfried + Riggins (Johns Hopkins glioma Phase I). Care Oncology METRICS trial (100 mg/day). "Better bioavailability, stronger microtubule binding." Often from integrative clinics.

Both hit microtubules and glutamine/glycolysis pathways similarly. Mebendazole is easier for an oncologist to discuss (human drug, clinical trial history). Fenbendazole is what most Facebook groups use (Tippens dose). Neither is proven for Greg's specific cancer.

"Farm Supply Is Better" — True or False?

Facebook sellers (Skymeds, Sanare Lab, Tractor Supply advocates) often push one of two narratives:

Claim A: "Panacur C from the farm store is the original Joe Tippens protocol — purest."

What's trueWhat's misleading
Joe Tippens used Panacur C (Merck Animal Health). Each 1-gram packet contains 222 mg fenbendazole. Each packet is only ~22% active drug — the rest is inactive excipients (polymer binders, flavoring). Compounding sites cite ~77% non-active filler. [Sanare Lab Panacur analysis]
Farm supply is cheap and widely available (Tractor Supply, Amazon, feed stores). Not labeled for human use. Dose accuracy depends on weighing granules. Batch quality varies. Not "better" — just cheaper and unregulated for humans.
Tippens took it with fatty food for absorption. Tippens was also on a Keytruda trial at MD Anderson — remission cannot be credited to Panacur alone. [mycancerstory.rocks]

Verdict: Farm supply = authentic Tippens backstory, not medically superior.

Claim B: "Human mebendazole / compounded capsules are better — higher purity, better absorption."

What's trueWhat's misleading
Mebendazole is the human-approved sister drug. Better documented PK in people. [Cancers 2019] Some online "human fenbendazole" capsules (Skymeds, Sanare) are still veterinary drug repackaged — "research use only" disclaimers. $160–220+ with mixed shipping reviews.
Riggins/Hopkins and Care Oncology paths use mebendazole, not fenbendazole — easier for oncologists to discuss. "99% purity" lab claims are vendor marketing unless you receive a batch-matched Certificate of Analysis (CoA) and your doctor agrees.
Preclinical studies suggest mebendazole may bind tubulin more potently than fenbendazole in some cell lines. [Lai 2017 canine glioma] Integrative clinics selling "mebendazole is better" often want your business — same incentive as farm-supply influencers.

Verdict: Mebendazole = stronger institutional research path; compounded FBZ capsules = convenience/purity if CoA verified — neither replaces chemo or oncologist approval.

Claim C: "Avoid farm supply — buy our capsules instead."

Verdict: Sales pitch. Online vendors (Skymeds, Sanare Lab, etc.) profit either way. Joe Tippens warns about counterfeit and scam groups — only his official Facebook group is endorsed. [My Cancer Story Rocks]
