I was researching. This is the good information, in one short place. You do not have to do more today.

Fat is the main food. Protein is a side, not a big plate of meat. You’re on the right track with bone broth.

If he can eat

  • Eggs, fish, or a little clean meat — not a huge portion
  • Always pair it with fat: olive oil, avocado, butter, or ghee

If eating is hard

  • Bone broth is a good protein. Add butter if he tolerates it.
  • A shake only if he cannot get enough food — unsweetened pea or rice, no sugar. Brands can be checked later.

Skip

  • Sugar, soda, juice, bread, pasta, rice
  • Turning this into a carnivore / big-meat diet

One clarification

  • We do not cut protein to “turn off” something in the body. The body makes that on its own.
  • We give enough protein to protect muscle, and use fat for most of the calories.
  • If he is losing weight or muscle, we do not underfeed him to chase a number.
