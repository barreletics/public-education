Main line: Keep carbs and glucose very low. Healthy fats are the primary calorie source. Protein is moderate — enough to maintain muscle, not the dominant calorie source. Don’t replace carbs with huge amounts of meat; replace most of those calories with healthy fat and protect his muscle.

Carbs

No carbs or sugar unless it’s an avocado or something that’s mostly fat.

Protein

  • Whole food when he can tolerate it: eggs, fish, clean meats — paired with healthy fats (avocado, olive oil, butter, ghee).
  • You’re on the right track with bone broth, especially when eating is hard. Add butter to bring fat up without more protein or carbs. Butter in tea works the same way.
  • If he must use a supplement (cachexia or cannot tolerate solid food): unsweetened bone broth protein (lower glutamine than whey/dairy) or clean unsweetened pea/rice protein — no added sugar, very low carbs. I can check brands I know.
  • Protein is a side, not a large plate of steak or chicken.

Fat

Fat is key. Roughly 80–90% of calories from fat in the therapeutic frame. Olive oil throughout the day on whatever he can eat; macadamia nut oil, MCT oil, avocado oil, ghee, butter.

Clarification (keep this)

Moderate protein is not about eliminating glutamine through diet — the body can make glutamine itself. The goal is enough protein to preserve lean muscle while keeping glucose and GKI controlled, with fat as the main calorie source. If he is losing weight or muscle, reassess protein and calories up — do not restrict harder to chase a number.

GKI

Glucose-Ketone Index: the relationship between blood glucose and ketones. Track glucose + ketones so you know how the diet is affecting him — not just assuming a food is “keto.”
