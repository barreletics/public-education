First — Understand What Cancer Actually Is

Cancer cells are metabolically broken. Unlike normal cells, they can't burn oxygen efficiently. So they do something desperate — they ferment, like yeast without air.

When cancer ferments, it creates toxic waste products: lactic acid and succinic acid.

Here's the critical part: Cancer deliberately dumps all this toxic waste around itself like a moat. This acidic shield does three things:

  • Blocks chemotherapy from reaching the tumor
  • Prevents your immune cells from attacking
  • Creates an invisible fortress that protects cancer from everything

This is why standard chemotherapy fails. Chemo can't penetrate the shield. It's like throwing darts at a moving target in the dark.

Here's How To Remove The Shield And Make Cancer Defenseless

Cancer fermentation requires TWO fuel sources:

  • GLUCOSE (sugar)
  • GLUTAMINE (amino acid in your blood)

The strategy is to cut off BOTH at the same time:

Step 1: Starve Glucose

Ketogenic diet (high fat, very low carbs) forces your body to burn fat instead. Your healthy cells switch to ketones — a different fuel source. Cancer cells cannot use ketones. They starve.

Step 2: Block Glutamine

Fenbendazole + ivermectin together are used in the community protocol to block cancer from accessing glutamine (the backup fuel). Seyfried's lab research shows mebendazole inhibits both glutamine and glucose pathways — especially when combined with a ketogenic diet (Cell Reports Medicine, 2026). Diet must come first; drugs are pulsed, not taken blindly at high dose forever.

Now cancer has no food at all.

Step 3: Watch The Shield Disappear

With no glucose and no glutamine coming in, cancer cannot ferment. It cannot make lactic acid and succinic acid anymore. The toxic moat is gone. The fortress collapses.

Now chemotherapy can actually reach the tumor. Now your immune system can actually attack. The tumor is completely exposed and defenseless.

Step 4: Measure You're In The Kill Zone

A simple Keto-Mojo meter ($20) tracks your glucose-ketone ratio (GKI). When you're at 1-3, you're in the cancer-killing metabolic state. You'll know it's working because you can measure it.
