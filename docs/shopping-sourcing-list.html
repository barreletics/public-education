Shopping & Sourcing List

Community protocol checklist for Greg. Confirm all drugs and supplements with Karmanos before starting — especially during chemo.

Veterinary fenbendazole is not FDA-approved for human cancer. Ivermectin for this use is off-label. This list reflects what patients report using — not a prescription. Human mebendazole (Rx) is the closest approved analog; discuss with oncology.

Tier 1 — Start before or with treatment

ItemTypical dose / useWhere patients sourceNotes
Keto-Mojo meter (+ glucose & ketone strips) 2× daily AM fasting + pre-dinner Amazon, keto-mojo.com, Walmart ~$40–50 starter; strips are ongoing cost. Required for GKI tracking.
Keto groceries <20 g net carbs/day Any grocery Eggs, avocados, olive oil, fatty fish, leafy greens, macadamia nuts, cheese, cauliflower. Zero sugar.
Electrolytes During keto adaptation Amazon, grocery Sodium, potassium, magnesium — keto flu is real. Nuun, LMNT, or bulk salts.

Tier 2 — Antiparasitic cycle (oncologist approval first)

Community cycle: 222 mg fenbendazole + 12 mg ivermectin, 4 days on / 10 days off, repeat. Take FBZ with a fatty meal.

ItemCommunity doseSourcing notesCautions
Fenbendazole (FBZ) 222 mg/day on cycle days Often Panacur C (22.2% granules) — patients weigh to 222 mg, or use pre-measured capsules from compounding/community vendors Veterinary product. Verify purity/source. Liver mets = monitor LFTs with oncology.
Ivermectin (IVM) 12 mg/day on cycle days Rx (human) preferred if physician will prescribe; some patients use veterinary sources (not recommended without medical oversight) Drug interactions possible with chemo — must ask oncologist.
Mebendazole (alternative) 100 mg/day (Care Oncology / METRICS style) or per physician Human Rx — ask oncologist or Care Oncology Clinic (UK) Seyfried/Riggins research path; may be easier for oncology to accept than veterinary FBZ.

Printable checklist

  • Keto-Mojo meter + strips (2 boxes)
  • Keto grocery staples (eggs, avocados, olive oil, fish, greens)
  • Electrolytes (sodium/potassium/magnesium)
  • Notebook or printed GKI sheet
  • Fenbendazole — source verified, dose measured (after oncologist OK)
  • Ivermectin — Rx preferred (after oncologist OK)
  • Vitamin E, curcumin, D3, magnesium
  • Pill organizer for 4-on / 10-off cycle
  • Fatty meal component for FBZ days (avocado, nut butter, etc.)

What NOT to buy yet

  • Bulk 90-day FBZ supply before oncologist conversation
  • High-dose supplement stacks from social media ads
  • "Cancer cure" proprietary blends with no ingredient list
  • DON (drug) or research chemicals — not DIY
Greg has liver metastases. Anything metabolized by the liver (FBZ, IVM, CBD, high-dose supplements) needs explicit oncology sign-off. When in doubt, wait until after Wednesday Aug 13.
