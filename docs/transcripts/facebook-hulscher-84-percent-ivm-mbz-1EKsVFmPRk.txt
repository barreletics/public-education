=== CAPTION (og:metadata) ===

BREAKING: Epidemiologist Nic Hulscher reports on a new peer-reviewed study of 200 cancer patients treated with compounded ivermectin + mebendazole.

After 6 months: 84.4% showed benefit — remission, tumor shrinkage, regression, or stabilization. Largest human study to date.

Now published in a major cancer journal.

=== THE PAPER ===
Title: Real-World Clinical Outcomes of Ivermectin and Mebendazole in Cancer Patients: Results from a Prospective Observational Cohort
Authors: Hulscher et al. (+ McCullough, Risch, Wellness Company affiliates)
Journal: Anticancer Research, Vol 46 No 6, pp 3243-3255, June 2026
DOI: https://doi.org/10.21873/anticanres.18194 (study)
Expression of Concern: https://doi.org/10.21873/anticanres.18276 (June 2026)
PubMed: PMID 42203321

=== WHAT THE STUDY ACTUALLY IS ===
- N=197 enrolled; N=122 completed 6-month follow-up (61.9% — 38% dropped out)
- **Observational cohort** — NOT randomized controlled trial
- **Self-reported outcomes** via digital surveys — not independently verified scans
- Intervention: compounded **25 mg ivermectin + 250 mg mebendazole** capsules (McCullough/Wellness Company protocol)
- **NOT fenbendazole** — uses human mebendazole
- Majority also on concurrent conventional therapies — not controlled for
- Cancer mix: prostate 27.9%, breast 18.3%, lung 8.6% — **no sinonasal NEC**

=== WHY IT SOUNDS "GOOD" ===
+ Actually published in peer-reviewed journal (Anticancer Research)
+ Larger n than Makis 3-patient retracted series
+ Uses **mebendazole** (human drug) — closer to Seyfried/Riggins lane than veterinary FBZ
+ 84.4% "clinical benefit ratio" includes stable disease, not just remission
+ Real paper exists — not pure Facebook fiction

=== WHY IT'S NOT KARMANOS-GRADE EVIDENCE ===
- Journal issued **Expression of Concern** (June 2026) citing:
  - Verifiability and statistical reliability questions
  - Ethical oversight / IRB concerns
  - Self-reported data insufficient for "84.4% tumor regression" claims
  - Post-publication audit initiated
- Authors tied to **Wellness Company** — sells the same drug combo (financial COI)
- Science-Based Medicine + Lead Stories: anonymous surveys, survivor bias, no control group
- Hulscher also promotes **retracted Makis fenbendazole** paper on social media — credibility mixed
- **Not retracted yet** (as of Aug 2026) but flagged
