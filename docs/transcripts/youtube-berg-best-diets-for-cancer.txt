=== LEGIT OR NOT? ===
**⚠️ Tier 3 educator — useful diet motivation, NOT oncology authority**

Legitimate in broad strokes (Warburg/metabolic framing, glucose/glutamine fuels, diet rotation concept) but:
- Berg is a **chiropractor (DC)**, not an MD or cancer researcher
- Cites papers he's "involved with" — not same weight as Seyfried lab publications
- No neuroendocrine carcinoma (NEC) in his cancer-type lists
- "Mortality rates unchanged for decades" is oversimplified — varies hugely by cancer type
- Free PDF is marketing/lead capture for Berg ecosystem
- Month-long fast anecdotes are not clinical evidence

Better primary source for Greg's metabolic lane: **Thomas Seyfried** (DOAC interview, MORE Alliance, GKI, mebendazole press-pulse)
